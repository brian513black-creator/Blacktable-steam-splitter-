# Blacktable Studio by Brian Privacy Policy

Last updated November 28, 2026. This policy covers Blacktable Studio version 0.1.3 for Windows and its support channel.

Blacktable Studio separates recordings into audio stems on your computer. The app opens in your default browser through a local address. It does not require an account. The app does not send your audio, stems, file names, or usage analytics to the publisher or an external service. It does not use advertising trackers.

When you select a recording, Blacktable copies it to a temporary folder on your computer. After a successful split it removes the source copy and working audio. The stems and ZIP files stay in session storage until normal shutdown. A forced shutdown may leave temporary files in the Windows temporary folder. The files you save stay in the location you choose in your browser. You can delete any saved exports or leftover temporary files in Windows.

Blacktable keeps a local diagnostic log in the Blacktable Studio folder under your Windows Local AppData folder. It may include local paths and error messages. The log is not sent automatically. You can delete it after closing the app. Temporary files and the log are stored under your Windows user account and rely on your computer's access controls.

If you contact support, the information you choose to provide is used to respond to your request. The public GitHub issues page is operated by GitHub and posts there can be visible to others. Please keep private recordings, personal information, and unredacted logs out of public posts. Microsoft Store installation and purchases, GitHub, and any separate features of your browser are subject to the respective providers’ policies.

For support or privacy questions, use the Blacktable Studio support page at https://github.com/brian513black-creator/Blacktable-steam-splitter-/issues.
